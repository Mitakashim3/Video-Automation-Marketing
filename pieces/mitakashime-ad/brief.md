# Mitakashime — "Sketch it in grey, build it in green" (26s ad)

## Spec
- **Brand:** Mitakashime | Business Automation Studio — "Business Automation, Done Your Way." — mitakashime.cosedevs.com
- **Audience:** non-technical owners of appointment-based small businesses (clinics, salons, repair and home services, gyms, tutors).
- **One true thing:** customers who message after hours and don't get a fast reply go elsewhere. Mitakashime first understands where a business loses people, then builds the system that replies in seconds and handles the rest.
- **Formats:** 1:1 1080×1080 (main, Facebook feed), 9:16 1080×1920 (Reels) — each laid out, not cropped.
- **Length / clock:** 26.0s, 24fps (624 frames), 120 BPM (8th = 6 frames), 13 bars.
- **Story format:** F4 mission (goal + helper, cut on the beat) — proven in the skill.
- **Voice:** none. Music only, composed in code. On-screen text carries the message.
- **Style:** new style from the user's references (`refs/`), saved later as `styles/mitakashime/`.
- **Hero:** the customer's message bubble — first frame to last.
- **Device (sayable):** *we sketch it in grey, then build it in green.* Dashed grey lines map the problem; the same lines turn solid green when the system is built.
- **Faceless:** no faces, no AI-generated footage, no competitor names or logos.

## Claims + sources
- "replied in 0:02" — the user's own product claim (their systems reply in seconds). No statistics or third-party claims on screen.

## Assets
- Logo files from `refs/` (the user's own), copied unaltered into `assets/`. Not redrawn or recoloured.
- No website screenshots; stations are drawn as abstract cards and nodes (no fake GoHighLevel UI).

## Avoid
- The v2 look: text fading in over static cards. Use camera moves, shape morphs, depth, springs, beat cuts.
- Neon glows, lens flares, busy decoration.
